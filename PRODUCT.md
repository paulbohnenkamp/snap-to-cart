# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Busy grocery shoppers using a phone at home, often while holding an empty package or moving through a kitchen. Their primary job is to turn a package photo into a trustworthy replenishment action with minimal typing.

## Product Purpose

Snap to Cart makes replenishing a known grocery item faster than searching for it manually. The MVP succeeds when a contributor can run the complete demo locally, recognize a package, review what was found, and receive an honest cart-action result.

## Brand Personality

Calm, capable, neighborly. The interface should feel trustworthy and direct, with useful guidance and no exaggerated claims about automation.

## Anti-references

Avoid coupon-heavy grocery marketplaces, gamified shopping flows, dense promotional feeds, and theatrical “AI magic” presentation. Do not imply that an external cart changed when the app only performed a demo action.

## Design Principles

- Keep the photo-to-action path focused and short.
- Make demo, pending, success, and failure states unmistakable.
- Confirm consequential actions with specific product and destination language.
- Prefer plain guidance over technical or promotional copy.
- Preserve user trust by describing only behavior that actually occurred.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Use minimum 44-point touch targets, explicit accessible names and states, non-color-only selection feedback, readable contrast, dynamic text-friendly layouts, and reduced-motion-safe interaction patterns.
